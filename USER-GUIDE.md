# 🍸 Last Call — How to Use It

Your AI bar concierge. Talk to it like a real bartender — tell it your mood or what
bottles you've got, and it'll pour you the perfect recommendation, then walk you
through making it **hands-free**, with the recipe, timers, and lighting right on screen.
Ask about **wine**, and it even hands you to a second expert — the **Sommelier** — who
takes over in her own voice, then hands you back to the bar when you're done.

---

## What it can — and can't — do

**It can:**
- 🍸 **Recommend real cocktails** — by mood ("something smoky and stirred"), by the bottles
  you have, by name, or a total surprise.
- 📋 **Show the recipe on screen** and **walk you through it step by step** while your
  hands are busy.
- ⏱️ **Run timers** for the shake/chill/steep (rings a bell), keep a **shopping list**
  (and **read it back** when you ask), and **set the room's mood** (a lighting theme you
  can say *or* click).
- 📚 **Answer technique & swap questions** ("shake or stir?", "what's a swap for triple
  sec?") from its bartending guide — and it'll tell you when it's going by the guide.
- 🙋 **Personalize** to your name, taste, and alcohol preference — **regular, low-ABV, or
  zero-proof** — and genuinely adapt the drink to it (lighter pours, zero-proof swaps).
- 🍷 **Talk wine** — ask for a pairing or a bottle and it hands you to the **Sommelier**,
  a second agent with a **different voice** and her own wine knowledge, then hands you back.
- 🌍 **Speak English or Spanish**, switching mid-conversation if you do.
- ⭐ **Save favorite drinks**, and generate a **recap after each call** — viewable on the
  host's private `/summary` page (see the last section).

**It won't:** take orders for delivery, discuss anything off-topic from drinks & hosting,
or push you to drink more — it'll happily steer you to water or a zero-proof option.

---

## Step up to the bar

1. **Open** the bar: **<https://ai-mixologist-elevenlabs.vercel.app>** (or `http://localhost:3000`
   if you're running it yourself). You'll land on the **bar dashboard**: the voice widget on
   the left, and live panels on the right (recipe card, timers, shopping list) that fill in as
   you talk.
2. *(Optional)* **Personalize your visit** — before connecting, fill the short form: your
   **name**, **taste** (e.g. "smoky and bitter"), **alcohol preference** (regular / low-ABV /
   zero-proof), and the **bottles you've got**. Skip it entirely and it still works great —
   it'll just greet you as "friend" and ask as it goes.
3. **Click "Step up to the bar"** and **allow your microphone** when the browser asks.
   The orb starts glowing — that means it's connected and listening.
4. **Just start talking.** It's a real back-and-forth: let it finish a thought, then reply.
   When you're done, say *"thanks, that's all"* — it gives a warm send-off and **hangs up
   for you**.

> 🎧 Headphones or a quiet room = the smoothest chat. Change your mind anytime:
> *"actually, make it a bit stronger,"* or *"one sec — I'm grabbing some ice"* (it'll wait quietly).

### 🍷 Meeting the Sommelier
Ask anything about **wine** — a food pairing, which bottle to buy, a grape, serving
temperature — and the bartender says a quick *"let me bring in our sommelier,"* then
**the voice changes**: a warmer, British sommelier voice (Lily) takes over. She already
knows what you've been chatting about, so you don't repeat yourself. Ask follow-ups
naturally (*"and if I switch to roast chicken?"*, *"what temperature should I pour a white at?"*,
*"suggest a bottle I can pick up tonight"*). When you want to go back to cocktails, just say so
(*"okay, back to cocktails — surprise me"*) — she hands you **back to the bar** and the
original voice resumes, still in context. It's all one call, one widget — only the voice
and expertise switch.

### Set the mood 🎨
Three lighting moods — **say it** (*"give the bar a speakeasy glow"*) **or click** the switcher in
the top corner:
- **Speakeasy** — warm, low-lit, classic
- **Tiki** — tropical teal & sunset orange
- **Bright** — clean and daytime-fresh

The switcher always highlights the current mood, and stays in sync whether you change it
by voice or by click.

### Drinking responsibly 🥂
Say *"go easy on the alcohol"* or *"make it zero-proof"* anytime and it genuinely adjusts the
drink — not just the words (a lighter pour, lengthened with soda, or a booze-free build) and
tells you how it lightened it. It sticks to bar talk, offers alcohol-free options happily, and
never pushes another round — no judgment, no lectures.

## A full visit, start to finish 🍹

Here's a complete sample session that shows off every part of the app. Step up to the
bar and say these one at a time — each line triggers something new, and the middle stretch
shows the **Sommelier handoff**.

**At the bar (the original voice):**
1. *"I've got mezcal and grapefruit — something smoky and bitter."*
   → it suggests a fitting cocktail from what you have, and says why it works.
2. *"Actually, go a little easier on the alcohol."*
   → it **lightens the drink** (smaller pour / lengthened) and tells you how.
3. *"Pull up the recipe and coach me through it."*
   → a **recipe card** slides in on the right (photo, measures, steps) and it paces you.
4. *"Time a 20-second shake."*
   → a live **countdown** appears and a **bell rings** 🔔 when it hits zero.
5. *"What's a good swap for triple sec?"*
   → a grounded answer **from its bartending guide** (it'll mention the house guide).
6. *"Add what I'm missing to my shopping list."* → the items drop into your **shopping list** panel.
7. *"Remind me what's on that list."* → it **reads the list back** to you, out loud.
8. *"Give the place a bright, daytime feel."* → the whole **room re-themes**.
9. *"Bookmark this one to my favorites."* → it keeps the drink in your favorites.

**Over to wine (the Sommelier's voice):**
10. *"Switching gears — I'm serving a mushroom risotto tonight. What wine?"*
    → the bartender hands off; **a new, British voice** greets you as the sommelier and
    suggests a pairing, citing the **house cellar notes** for the why.
11. *"And if I switch to roast chicken?"* → a different recommendation, explained.
12. *"What temperature should I pour a white at?"* → a grounded answer from the cellar notes.
13. *"Suggest a bottle I could pick up tonight."* → a concrete, friendly recommendation.

**Back to the bar:**
14. *"Okay, back to cocktails — surprise me with something fun."*
    → she hands you **back**; the **original voice resumes** (still in context) and pours you a
    **random** pick.
15. *"That's me for tonight — ¡muchas gracias!"* → it answers in kind and **hangs up** for you.

A recap of everything you just did is generated right after you hang up — the host can see it
on the private `/summary` page (see below).

> 💡 Your **shopping list** and **mood** are remembered across reloads; the recipe card and
> timers clear when you refresh.

## After the call: the recap 📋 (host view)
Once a conversation ends, ElevenLabs generates a short recap of the visit, shown on the
**`/summary`** page:
- a **plain-language summary** of what was talked about,
- the **drink landed on**, the guest's **taste** and **alcohol preference**, and whether they
  **actually made something**,
- two quality checks — was it a **complete, makeable recipe**, and was the service
  **responsible** (alcohol preference honored).

It appears a few seconds after the call (the recap is generated right after hangup).

> 🔒 **On the live site, `/summary` is private** — it's the host's analytics view (other
> guests' recaps + taste profiles live there), so it's behind a username/password. Running
> locally with `npm run dev`, it's open at `http://localhost:3000/summary`.
